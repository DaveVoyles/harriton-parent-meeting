👋 About Coach Dave

  • Only coach this year — looking for parent help and additional coaches We’re building the program with the people in this room. Extra hands on the field and behind the scenes make a real difference for the boys.
  • Fun, but tough and fair Practices and games should feel enjoyable — and every player will be held to clear, consistent standards.
  • May mean merging JV and Varsity into one program Depending on numbers and coaching support, we may run as one combined group so everyone gets better coaching and more meaningful reps.

✓ Player expectations

  • Every player must have their own equipment Stick, helmet, pads, gloves, and mouthguard — ready for practice and games. SidelineSwap is a fantastic place to buy gear at an affordable price — that's where Coach Dave gets his stuff. Ask if you need help finding anything.
  • Every player must be able to catch and throw Basic stick skills are the foundation. Wall ball and partner catch at home go a long way. If you cannot catch & throw, that will be your dedicated practice until you can.
  • Every player must be in shape Lacrosse is a running game. Showing up fit helps your son contribute safely and confidently from day one. We will be in better shape than any other team in Central League. That’s our strength.
  • Other spring sports Communicate with me if you plan on playing an additional sport in the spring. Be very clear about which is your priority. I encourage players to play multiple sports — however, it’s not fair to teammates if we are investing in you and you are not investing in the team.
  • Central League is among the best in the country We get as much out of our practices as we put in. The local competition is playing all year, every year, from the time they are in kindergarten. The advantage we have is we will be in better shape. I reward hustle.

🍂 Off Season

  • Fall practices: Saturdays, 10:00 AM–12:00 PM, at Strath Haven HS Coach Dave is not running these — the Strath Haven coach is. Sessions include players from several other schools. Great for skills and chemistry before winter and spring; please put them on the family calendar.
  • Players who will be 18 this year can sign up for Ulax Ulax plays in the winter at Seelyard Sports in Conshohocken — another strong option for older players who want extra reps.

★ Captains

Players nominate and select 3 captains + 1 non-senior.

  • Represent the team, communicate with Coach Dave, and take on delegated tasks Captains are the four people who best speak for the group — on the field, in the locker room, and in conversations with the coach.
  • The non-senior builds leadership for next year’s class That seat is intentional: it keeps continuity when seniors graduate and gives younger leaders real responsibility this season.
For players: Open captain nomination form Players nominate captains here. No parent email required. Useful for parents to see how the vote works.
